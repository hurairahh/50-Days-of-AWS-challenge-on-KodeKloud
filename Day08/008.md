Day 08 of 50 Days of AWS Challenge on KodeKloud.
Today’s task focused on EC2 instance protection.
The task involved enabling Stop Protection for the instance in the us-east-1 region.

-- AWS client commands used
aws ec2 describe-instance-attribute \
 --instance-id i-07de5bdd7193a7cb0 \
 --attribute disableApiStop

What is Stop Protection?
Stop Protection is an EC2 feature that helps prevent an instance from being accidentally stopped through the AWS console or API. When enabled, the instance cannot be stopped until the protection is disabled.

Why is it useful?
It adds an extra layer of protection for important or critical instances, reducing the risk of accidental downtime.
