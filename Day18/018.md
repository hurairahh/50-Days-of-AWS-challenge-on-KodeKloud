Day 18 of 50 Days of AWS Challenge on KodeKloud.
The task involved creating an IAM policy named iampolicy_john in the us-east-1 region to provide read-only access to the EC2 console.

What is an IAM Policy?
An IAM policy is a document that defines what actions a user, group, or role is allowed or denied to perform on AWS resources.

What does read-only EC2 access mean?
A read-only policy allows users to view EC2 resources and their configurations without giving them permission to create, modify, or delete instances and other resources.
